🚀 How to Install as Farcaster Mini App:

  1. Copy this URL: https://scrabbly-frame.vercel.app/mini-app.html
  2. Open Farcaster (Warpcast app or web)
  3. Go to Mini Apps in the sidebar
  4. Click "Add Mini App" or "+" button
  5. Paste the URL and install
  6. Start playing! ScraBBly will appear in your Mini Apps
